Our story

One family store, five departments

Hibretfamily ("hibret" — unity, in Tigrinya) started as a single neighborhood shop in Asmara and grew into a full department store: fashion and shoes for every age, electronics for the home, books for curious minds, and cosmetics for everyday confidence. Whatever the errand, it's one trip — online or at any of our stores.
