How it works

Three steps, one seller at a time

Browse & add to cart

Every product is listed and priced by an independent seller. Your cart holds one seller's items at a time, since each checkout pays that seller directly.

Secure checkout

Stripe processes your payment and instantly splits it: the seller's share goes straight to their own account, and Hibretfamily keeps its commission — no wallet, no holding period.

Delivery & returns

Your order ships directly from that seller, under their own delivery and return policy. Your receipt and order confirmation arrive by email.

Our story

From Asmara's avenues to a global marketplace

Hibretfamily ("hibret" — unity, in Tigrinya) takes its name and spirit from the streets that shape Eritrea's story: Harnet Avenue and Sematat Avenue in Asmara, and the historic Red Sea port of Massawa. Today it connects independent Eritrean sellers — fashion, shoes, electronics, books, cosmetics and more — with buyers everywhere, while every seller gets paid directly and instantly for their own sales.
