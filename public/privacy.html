Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Hibretfamily ("we", "us") operates hibretfamily.com, a marketplace connecting independent sellers in Eritrea with buyers. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how we protect it.

Information we collect

  • Buyers: name, email, phone number, delivery address and preferences, and payment details, provided when you check out or contact us. Payment card details are handled directly by Stripe and never stored on our servers.
  • Sellers: business name, email, and payout details (Stripe account, or mNakfa number, account holder name, and zoba/region), provided when you register to sell.
  • Usage data: basic technical information (browser type, pages visited) collected automatically to keep the site working and improve it.

How we use it

  • To process orders, payments, and deliveries
  • To send order confirmations and delivery updates (by email, SMS, or WhatsApp)
  • To respond to questions sent through our contact channels
  • To keep the marketplace secure and prevent fraud

Sharing

We share information only with the services needed to run the marketplace: Stripe (payments), Supabase (database hosting), Twilio (SMS/WhatsApp notifications), and the specific seller fulfilling your order. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Advertising

Pages on this site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by reaching us through the contact details in the footer below.
