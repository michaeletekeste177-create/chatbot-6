# Hibretfamily — Promotional Video Script

**Working title:** "Ne'alem" (To the World) / ንዓለም
**Total runtime:** 60 seconds
**Languages:** Tigrinya (primary voiceover) with English subtitles, or as two separate localized cuts
**Scope:** All cities in Eritrea — footage should not be limited to Asmara and Massawa alone; b-roll from Keren, Mendefera, and other cities is encouraged if available, even though the three scripted scenes below focus on Asmara and Massawa as the platform's founding landmarks.

This is a hand-off document for a video editor/production team — it is not itself a video. Timings are targets, not exact cut points; adjust to the pacing of the actual footage.

---

## Scene 1 — Cold open (0:00–0:15)

**Visual:** Massawa's ancient coral-stone architecture and the Red Sea at golden hour, transitioning (hard cut or cross-dissolve) to Harnet Avenue in Asmara — its Italian-modernist facades, palm-lined median, evening foot traffic.

**Audio (voiceover):**

| Language | Line |
|---|---|
| Tigrinya | ታሪኽ፣ ባህልን ዓለምለኸ ንግድን ዝራኸቡሉ ፍሉይ ማእከል—ኤርትራ! |
| English | Where history, culture, and global trade unite—Eritrea! |

**Notes:** Establishing shot energy — wide aerials or slow dolly, warm color grade. Music: builds from a single traditional instrument (krar or masenqo) into a fuller arrangement by the end of the line.

---

## Scene 2 — The product (0:15–0:45)

**Visual:** Traditional clothing and handmade crafts, shown across multiple Eritrean cities (market stalls, artisan workshops, a tailor at work). Cut to a phone or tablet showing the Hibretfamily app/site UI, landing on the "የቐንየልና / Thank You" confirmation screen after a purchase.

**Audio (voiceover):**

| Language | Line |
|---|---|
| Tigrinya | ካብ ፍሉያት ባህላዊ ፍርያትና ክሳብ ዘደንቁ ናይ ቱሪዝም ቦታታትና፤ ሕጂ ንመድረኽና ብምጥቃም ንዓለም ነላልዮ። ውሑስ፣ ቅልጡፍን ምዱብን ዕዳጋ! |
| English | From unique cultural treasures to breathtaking tourism destinations; we bring Eritrea to the world. Safe, fast, and seamless marketplace! |

**Notes:** This is the longest scene (30s) — budget for 4–6 distinct shots: (1) artisan hands at work, (2) a finished product, (3) a tourism/landscape cutaway, (4) the app UI, (5) the "Thank You" confirmation screen held for at least 2 seconds so it reads clearly, (6) a seller smiling at a delivery/pickup moment. Keep the UI screen recording clean — capture it from the actual site once real product photography is loaded, not a placeholder.

---

## Scene 3 — Call to action (0:45–1:00)

**Visual:** Hibretfamily logo animates in over a soft brand-color background; the site URL appears; a "Register" button/CTA is highlighted (matches the `sell.html` registration flow).

**Audio (voiceover):**

| Language | Line |
|---|---|
| Tigrinya | ሕጂ ተመዝገቡ፣ ንግድኹም ምስ ዓለም ኣተሓሕዙ! |
| English | Register today and connect your business to the global market! |

**Notes:** End card should hold for at least 3 seconds after the voiceover ends — long enough to read the URL and screenshot the CTA. Match the "Register as a Seller" wording used on the actual `sell.html` page so the video and the landing page feel like one continuous experience.

---

## Production notes (not in the original brief, offered as practical guidance)

- **Aspect ratios:** cut both a 16:9 (YouTube/web) and a 9:16 (Instagram/TikTok Stories) version if this will run on social — the shot list above works for both if framed with a center-safe area in mind.
- **On-screen text:** burn in the site's actual `sell.html` URL during Scene 3 so it survives platforms that strip video descriptions.
- **Subtitle sync:** if cutting a single bilingual version rather than two localized ones, prefer Tigrinya audio with English burned-in subtitles (or vice versa) rather than talking over both — cleaner for accessibility and less busy visually.
- **Rights:** confirm usage rights/model releases for any real sellers, artisans, or locations filmed before this goes live, especially for Scene 2's seller/tourism footage.
